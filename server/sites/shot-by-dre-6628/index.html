Behind the camera

I'm a beauty and portrait photographer based in Wuse, Abuja. I shoot birthdays, couples, families and studio beauty portraits, working with makeup artists and stylists to get every detail right. Come as you are and leave with images you'll want to frame.

  2. Plan the look
